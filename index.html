How Tinker’s works

  1. Pick your hall from the palette button in the top corner — Vintage, Modern or Futuristic. Each changes the whole look and the band, instantly.
  2. The front runners, right at the top of the page, keep an eye on the three boards nearest the house — they update the moment each ball lands.
  3. Print the 30 boards — one to a full A4 page each — and hand them round, or just follow along on screen.
  4. Press “Eyes down”. The drum draws 0 to 80 with no repeats, and the caller shouts every number with its proper bingo call.
  5. When a line is won, the caller stops. He announces the winning board, then the hall takes a breather — check the board, make a fuss, and press “Carry on for the house” when you’re ready to play on.
  6. The full house ends the game — winning board number announced loud and clear, confetti and all. “Play again” wipes the daubs and plays a fresh draw on the very same boards.

Accessibility & comfort: three hall modes — Vintage, Modern and Futuristic (neon) — plus a High Contrast option for anyone with low vision, all from the palette button in the top corner. The band automatically dips down while the caller speaks, and each mode has its own music. The caller’s voice and sound effects each have their own switch, every call is captioned on screen, the flash board updates live, and the game respects your reduced-motion setting.
