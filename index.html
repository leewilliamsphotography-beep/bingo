About Our Home

A home where everyone is known, not just cared for.

Tinkers Hatch is more than a care home — it's a community. Set on New Pond Hill, surrounded by gardens, orchards, and quiet walking paths, we support adults with learning disabilities to live full, meaningful, and connected lives.

Our approach is built on relationships. We take time to learn each person's rhythm, preferences, and dreams. Whether that means tending the vegetable patch, joining a local art class, or simply enjoying a cup of tea in the conservatory — every day is shaped by the people who live here.

What We Believe

Four simple values, lived every day.

These aren't words on a poster. They shape how we greet each other in the morning, how we plan our weeks, and how we make decisions — large and small.

Kindness First

Every interaction begins with patience and warmth. We assume good intent, listen carefully, and respond with care.

Belonging

Everyone has a place here. We celebrate differences, share meals, and build a community where no one is on the outside.

Choice & Voice

Residents shape their days. From what's for breakfast to which activities we run — choices are real, and voices are heard.

Growing Together

We learn, try new things, and celebrate small wins. Growth looks different for everyone — and that's the point.

A Day at Tinkers Hatch

A gentle rhythm, not a rigid schedule.

Here's a sense of how a typical day flows. Each person chooses what to join — and there's always room for a quiet moment.

Morning

Slow starts with breakfast cooked to order. Some head to the garden, others prefer a quiet cuppa in the conservatory. We come together for a 10am circle to share plans for the day.

Midday

Activities in full swing — baking in the kitchen, or a walk down to the village. Lunch is shared around big tables, with fresh food prepared lovingly by our chef Joe.

Afternoon

A slower pace. Reading, music, visits from family, or time spent in a favourite chair. Tea and cake at 3.30 — everyone's welcome.

Evening

Supper together, then a film, a board game, or simply winding down. Bedtimes are flexible — this is home, and home runs on your own clock.

Good to Know

Questions, Answered.

Thinking about Tinkers Hatch for a loved one? Here are some of the things families ask us most.

Can residents bring their own furniture?

Absolutely. We want Tinkers Hatch to feel like home, not a hotel. Residents are encouraged to bring their own beds, wardrobes, pictures, and treasured belongings to make their space uniquely theirs.

How do you handle medical appointments and medication?

Our trained keyworkers oversee all medication management in a safe, regulated way. We also accompany residents to medical appointments and keep families fully informed of any health updates or doctor recommendations.

What is the difference between full-time and respite care?

Full-time clients live at Tinkers Hatch permanently. Respite clients stay with us for shorter periods — perhaps while their usual family caregiver takes a holiday or needs a break. Both receive the exact same level of care, activities, and warmth.

Are pets allowed to visit?

Yes! We know how important animal companionship is. Well-behaved dogs on leads are very welcome to visit the garden. We also regularly have therapy animals visit the home.
